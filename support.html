HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap START to begin a round, then touch anywhere on the screen to bite. The trap stays shut until you let go.
  • Bite when bugs have landed on the leaves: everything that has landed gets eaten.
  • Hold the trap shut and the plant closes its mouth. The game waits for you until you let go.

TIPS

  • Every bug in one bite multiplies your score, so waiting for a crowd pays.
  • A bug shivers just before it flies off. Take it right then for a JUST IN TIME bonus.
  • Beetles need cracking first: it takes more than one bite to eat one.
  • Never bite a wasp or a hornet. They cost you hearts. Shake your phone to make them let go, but it startles everything else too.
  • Biting at nothing costs two seconds off the clock (or food in Endless). Chill never charges.
  • In the timed levels, running out of hearts means the round scores nothing, so stay alive.

LEVELS

Five levels from Chill to Swarm, each a 60-second round. The game waits while you hold the trap shut, so you can take a breather. Endless has no clock: you have three hearts, and the plant gets hungrier the longer you go. Keep it fed, or it starves.

LEADERBOARDS AND ACHIEVEMENTS

Only Swarm and Endless are ranked, on Game Center. See where you stand among friends or the whole world, today, this week or all time. Tap LEADERBOARDS on the menu.

There are 14 achievements, a few of them secret. You'll find them under LEADERBOARDS › ACHIEVEMENTS. Anything you earn while you're not signed in to Game Center is sent the next time you are.

ADS AND REMOVE ADS

Hungry Flytrap is free, and an ad plays after each round. Tap REMOVE ADS on the menu to get rid of them for good with a one-time purchase.

Already bought it? Tap Restore purchase under the button, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

In places where the law asks for it, you'll see a choice about how ads can use your data. You can change it any time with Ad privacy choices on the menu, and you can turn tracking off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

QUESTIONS

My score isn't on the leaderboard. Make sure you're signed in under Settings › Game Center, and that it was a Swarm or Endless round. A Swarm round that ends with no hearts left scores nothing, so there's nothing to send. New scores can take a moment to show up.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads. Tap Restore purchase on the menu while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I reset my best scores? Best scores are kept on your device, so deleting and reinstalling the game clears them. Scores and achievements already on Game Center stay there.

HOW TO PLAY

  • On iPhone Duo: open the phone, then fold it about halfway shut to bite. Open it again to let the jaws spring back. Folding is also how a round starts.
  • On every other iPhone and on iPad: tap START to begin a round, then touch anywhere on the screen to bite. The trap stays shut until you let go.

TIPS

  • Bite when bugs have landed on the leaves. Every bug in one bite multiplies your score, so waiting for a crowd pays.
  • A bug shivers just before it flies off. Take it right then for a JUST IN TIME bonus.
  • Beetles need cracking first: it takes more than one bite to eat one.
  • Never bite a wasp or a hornet. They cost you hearts. Shake the phone to make them let go, but it startles everything else too.
  • Biting at nothing costs two seconds off the clock (or food in Endless). Chill never charges.
  • In the timed levels, running out of hearts means the round scores nothing, so stay alive.

LEVELS

Five levels from Chill to Swarm, each a 60-second round. The game waits while the plant is shut (the phone folded closed, or your finger held down), so you can take a breather. Endless has no clock: you have three hearts, and the plant gets hungrier the longer you go. Keep it fed, or it starves.

LEADERBOARDS

Only Swarm and Endless are ranked, on Game Center. See where you stand among friends or the whole world, today, this week or all time.

Folding and tapping are different games, so iPhone Duo has leaderboards of its own, and every other iPhone and every iPad share the other set. Your scores go to your own device's set, but you can look at either from any device: use the IPHONE DUO | IPHONE & IPAD switch at the top of the leaderboards screen.

QUESTIONS

My score isn't on the leaderboard. Make sure you're signed in under Settings › Game Center, and that it was a Swarm or Endless round. A Swarm round that ends with no hearts left scores nothing, so there's nothing to send. New scores can take a moment to show up.

I can't see my friend on the leaderboard. If one of you plays on an iPhone Duo and the other doesn't, you're in different leagues. Flip the switch at the top of the leaderboards screen to see theirs.

How do I reset my best scores? Best scores are kept on your device, so deleting and reinstalling the game clears them. Scores already on Game Center stay there.

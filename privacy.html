HUNGRY FLYTRAP

PRIVACY POLICY
HOMESUPPORT

Hungry Flytrap, by Roozbeh Moayyedian. Effective October 5, 2026.

The short version: Hungry Flytrap doesn't collect your personal information, doesn't track you, and has no ads, accounts or servers of its own. Your scores stay on your device, and if you're signed in to Game Center, your Swarm and Endless scores go to Apple's leaderboards.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

The game remembers your best score for each level, the level you picked last, whether the sound is muted, and whether you've learned to shake wasps off. These are stored only on your device and are deleted if you delete the app. They're never sent to me or anyone else.

MOTION SENSORS

The game reads your device's motion sensors so the plant's googly eyes roll with gravity and so it can tell when you shake the device. On iPhone Duo it also reads the hinge angle, because folding the phone is how the plant bites. All of this is used instantly on your device to run the game. It's never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

GAME CENTER

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores from the Swarm and Endless levels to Game Center leaderboards, and shows those leaderboards in the game, including other players' nicknames and scores. Game Center is run by Apple: your Game Center nickname, avatar and scores are visible to other players according to your Game Center settings, and Apple's Game Center privacy information explains how Apple handles that data. The game only displays leaderboard entries; it doesn't save or share them.

You can play every part of the game without signing in to Game Center, and you can sign in or out at any time under Settings › Game Center.

ADVERTISING, ANALYTICS AND TRACKING

This version of Hungry Flytrap contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. If a future version adds any of these, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and you'll be asked for permission wherever the law or Apple requires it.

CHILDREN

Hungry Flytrap doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
